# Community code of conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect in issues, pull requests, reviews, and discussions. Welcome people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Give feedback about the change, explain technical disagreements, and leave room for corrections.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory attacks, sexual harassment, spam, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable. Do not post credentials, private prompts, or personal account details.

The maintainer, [@sep1ol](https://github.com/sep1ol), may remove content, close or lock conversations, or restrict participation to enforce this policy. Decisions should address the behavior and its effect on the community.

For repository moderation, contact the maintainer in a discussion without repeating sensitive material. Use GitHub's reporting tools on the offending content for threats, harassment, or exposure of private information. Security vulnerabilities belong in the private channel described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
